Summary :
This data analysis examines the relationships between various course characteristics, such as Course Category, Time Spent on Course, Number of Videos Watched, Device Type, and their impact on completion rates. The dataset consists of 9000 entries, each representing a unique user's course completion data.

Key Findings :
Course Category: The analysis reveals that courses in Arts and Programming have the highest completion rates, while courses in Health have the lowest completion rates.

Time Spent on Course: The average time spent on course is 50.16 hours, with a significant positive correlation between time spent and completion rate.

Number of Videos Watched: The average number of videos watched is 10.02, with a moderate positive correlation between video watching and completion rate.

Quiz Scores: The average quiz score is 74.71, with a significant positive correlation between quiz scores and completion rate.

Completion Rate: The overall completion rate is 39.64%, with a significant positive correlation between time spent, video watching, and quiz scores.

Overall, these insights can help educators and course designers tailor their instruction to better meet the needs of students in different course categories, ultimately improving student outcomes and completion rates.
